# Neuroo conversion recovery plan

Updated 2026-09-02.

## What the current numbers actually say

App Store Connect, last 90 days through September 1:

- 6,779 impressions, 198 product-page views, and 42 total downloads.
- 38 first-time downloads, 4 redownloads, and a detailed conversion rate of 0.78%.
- App Store Search: 34 downloads at 0.67% conversion.
- App Store Browse: 5 downloads at 6.00% conversion.
- App Referrer: 3 downloads at 42.86% conversion. Treat this as the owner's friend-link sample, not a repeatable benchmark.

Google Search Console, August 25–31:

- 300 impressions, 0 clicks, and average position 36.2.
- The homepage generated 146 impressions; `/memory-test` generated 104.

The 0.78% figure is blended across cold App Store impressions, not a clean “people who watched a video and opened the page” funnel. A 20–30% rate is a reasonable target for warm, qualified product-page traffic; it is not a realistic immediate target for all App Store Search impressions.

## Changes made

- The public site now says iPhone only, matching the shipped app. It no longer leads with “Memory Test.”
- The number-memory SEO page remains available for search demand, but its App Store CTA now uses a separate `memory_test` campaign token.
- A `Social Challenge` Custom Product Page draft is prepared in App Store Connect with five real iPhone screens, games-first ordering, concise copy, and relevant keywords.
- The existing short profile routes remain the right place for clickable links. Spoken and on-screen CTAs say: “Find Neuroo on the iOS App Store.”

## The next measurement loop

1. Put the approved Social Challenge page behind the short social profile routes.
2. Publish 8–12 short videos using one clear challenge per video: show the game, show the choice, show the result, then say “Find Neuroo on the iOS App Store.”
3. Review each social campaign separately after Apple has enough data: product-page views, impressions, first-time downloads, and conversion rate.
4. Keep the winning hook and game; replace weak hooks weekly. Optimize for completion, shares, profile visits, and downloads—not likes.
5. Only start Product Page Optimization after the page has enough qualified traffic for Apple’s estimate to fit inside its 90-day window. At the current volume, Apple estimated a 25% lift test would take about 1,249 weeks, so a global test now would produce noise rather than an answer.

## Store-page hypothesis

The current winning hypothesis is simple: people need to understand the product in one glance. “15+ brain training games” and the real Games screen should beat a memory-only first impression because it matches the product promise and the content people are seeing. The daily-workout and progress screens then answer “why keep it?”

Success criteria for the first qualified social batch:

- Social product-page conversion reaches 15% first, then 20–30% with iteration.
- The page produces at least 10 first-time downloads before judging the result.
- At least one hook produces repeatable profile visits and shares, not just a one-off spike.
