# Neuroo organic content system

Updated 2026-08-22. The goal is not to make Neuroo look like a generic “brain test.” It is to show a small, satisfying daily habit: open the app, play a few quick games, and see progress build.

## Link system

Use a branded, human-readable URL everywhere. Do not paste an App Store URL into a caption or profile.

| Channel | Public link | Apple campaign token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube Shorts | `getneuroo.com/youtube` | `youtube_organic` |
| Instagram Reels | `getneuroo.com/instagram` | `instagram_organic` |
| Facebook | `getneuroo.com/facebook` | `facebook_organic` |
| TikTok | `getneuroo.com/tiktok` | `tiktok_organic` |
| Website / general | `getneuroo.com/download` | `site` |

These short routes redirect to the Organic Social Custom Product Page and keep the channel source in Apple’s campaign field. Keep the visible link exactly as written; the long App Store URL stays behind it.

## Four content pillars

1. **Can you beat this?** A 5–12 second challenge: show a number, pattern, color sequence, or odd one out, then reveal the answer.
2. **Game in one sentence.** Screen-record one real Neuroo round. Explain the rule in plain English and show the difficulty increasing.
3. **Three-minute habit.** Show the daily workout, one game from each skill, and the Brain Score or streak changing.
4. **Brain-training education.** Explain a useful idea—chunking, attention switching, or why short consistent practice is easier to keep—without promising medical outcomes.

## Four-week posting cadence

Post 4 short videos per week to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels. Reuse the same clean 9:16 master; change the first line and caption to fit the channel.

| Week | Monday | Wednesday | Friday | Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Number Memory challenge | “What’s missing?” game | 3-minute daily workout | Brain Score tour |
| 2 | Color Clash reaction test | Codebreaker round | Memory vs focus: what changed? | Streak / habit reset |
| 3 | Name Match challenge | Odd One Out speed round | How adaptive difficulty works | 15 games, 4 skills |

## Ready-to-record scripts

### 1. Number Memory

**Hook:** “How many digits can you hold for three seconds?”

Show a sequence, cut to the empty input, then reveal the answer. End with: “Neuroo has this plus 14 more quick brain training games. `getneuroo.com/youtube`.”

### 2. What’s Missing?

**Hook:** “One shape disappears. Can you spot it before the timer runs out?”

Show the board, remove one item, give viewers one beat to answer, then show the app result. Keep the first frame visual; do not lead with a logo or a paragraph.

### 3. Codebreaker

**Hook:** “Four colors. One code. How fast can you crack it?”

Screen-record one real attempt and overlay only the rules: “Guess → get clues → solve.” Finish on the personal-best screen.

### 4. Three-minute workout

**Hook:** “No hour-long brain workout—just today’s three games.”

Show the daily plan, one quick beat from each game, and the completed workout. CTA: “Try the routine free at `getneuroo.com/instagram`.”

### 5. Chunking tip

**Hook:** “The easiest way to remember a longer number is to stop seeing seven digits.”

Group a number into 2–3 digit chunks, then try the same sequence in Number Memory. Say: “Practice the strategy, not a medical promise.”

### 6. Brain Score

**Hook:** “Your score is not just your fastest game.”

Show the Brain Score screen and label the four inputs: consistency, variety, volume, skill. End with a score movement or streak—not a claim about intelligence.

## Caption template

`Can you beat this one? [one-sentence challenge]. Neuroo is quick brain training games for memory, focus, logic & reasoning—built for a few minutes a day. Try it: getneuroo.com/[channel]`

Use 3–5 specific hashtags, not a wall of tags: `#braintraining #memorygame #puzzlegame #focus #neuroo`.

## Weekly scorecard

Record this every Monday for the previous 7 days:

- Views, 3-second views, average watch time, and completion rate per video.
- Saves/shares and profile visits; these matter more than likes for this product.
- Redirect visits by route (`/youtube`, `/instagram`, `/facebook`, `/tiktok`).
- App Store Connect: product-page views, impressions, and app units by campaign token.
- GSC: clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for `/`, `/memory-test`, and `/vs/lumosity`.
- Firebase: `sign_up`, `game_started`, `game_completed`, `paywall_viewed`, `subscribe_tapped`, and `purchase_result`.

Keep a post only if it earns a clear next test. After 12–16 posts, make the first Product Page Optimization test with the winning premise as the treatment; do not start an A/B test before there is enough traffic to read it.
