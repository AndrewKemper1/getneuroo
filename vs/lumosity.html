The real difference

Lumosity is built like a program: dashboards, percentile indexes, long training arcs. That depth is impressive on a features page — and it's also why sessions feel like homework. Neuroo strips all of that down to the part that actually works: open the app, play today's three games, watch one number go up. The training only helps if you show up tomorrow, and Neuroo is engineered around exactly that.

Neither app is a medical device, and no brain training app should promise to prevent cognitive decline. What Neuroo promises is simpler: quick, genuinely fun games that get harder as you improve, and an honest score that reflects the work you put in.

One honest caveat: Neuroo is iPhone & iPad only. If you need Android or browser training, Lumosity has it — and if you're on an iPhone, that difference costs you nothing.
