The philosophy difference

Lumosity is built like a research platform — deep metrics, percentile indexes, long program arcs. Neuroo is built like a habit: open the app, get today's three games picked around your goals, watch one number go up. Both approaches train the same underlying skills — memory, attention, logic, reasoning. The question is which one you'll actually open every day.

Neither app is a medical device, and no brain training app should promise to prevent cognitive decline. What Neuroo promises is simpler: quick, genuinely fun games that get harder as you improve, and an honest score that reflects the work you put in.
