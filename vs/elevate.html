Does Elevate Brain Training actually train memory?

Elevate's best-known exercises are about language: spotting grammar errors, expanding your vocabulary, reading faster, speaking more precisely. It's genuinely good at that, and if your goal is to write better email, it's the right purchase.

But most people who go looking for "brain training" aren't worried about their sentence construction. They're worried that they walked into a room and forgot why, that they read a page and absorbed nothing, that they blanked on a name ten seconds after hearing it. That's memory and attention — and it's the whole of what Neuroo trains.
