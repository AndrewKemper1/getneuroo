Questions, answered

Is Neuroo free?

Yes — Neuroo is free to download and includes free games you can play every day. Neuroo Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks all 15 games, removes ads, and adds full stats & history — with a 7-day free trial.

How long does a session take?

A daily plan is 3 short games — most people finish in a few minutes. Every game is built for quick rounds, so it fits a coffee break.

What is the Brain Score?

A single number out of 1000 that blends Consistency (showing up daily), Variety (training different skills), Volume (how much you train) and Skill (how far you push each game). It rewards steady training, not just one lucky high score.

What devices does Neuroo support?

Neuroo is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.

Does brain training make me smarter?

Neuroo games are designed to challenge memory, attention, logic and reasoning in short, progressively harder rounds — and to make daily mental exercise a habit you keep. Neuroo is a training and entertainment app, not a medical device.
